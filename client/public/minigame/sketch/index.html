    How to play
    1. Join the game, and once at least two people are in, anyone can press Start game.
    2. Each round one player draws a secret word that only they can see, with no letters or numbers.
    3. Everyone else watches the drawing appear live and types guesses. You get 75 seconds a round.
    4. The first correct guess scores a point, and so does the drawer. If two guesses land within half a second, both score.
    5. Everyone takes a turn drawing, then the highest score wins.
