# Crit 9 reflection

## The breakthrough that moved the work forward

Free assets and Gemini. Free assets let me build part of the ANU campus and
its floor and wall textures, and Gemini made the rest: the sky, the textures
I wasn't happy with, and the background music. After this week I thought:
wow, one person can make a game now, and what will matter is having creative
ideas. I think the developers who last will be the ones who use several AIs
well, each at the right moment.

Working out which model does what was the other half. I only realised last
week that I could use Opus. This week I wrote the split into
[`docs/roles.md`](../docs/roles.md): Sonnet at medium effort for simple jobs,
Opus at high effort for careful reasoning. It made the work faster and saved
credits at the same time.

## What this changed about who I want to be as a developer

Why did I turn from a poop into ANU? Poop is a good subject for strangers to
get close over, but I wanted to go beyond just pooping and make something with
real use: something people can actually interact in, worth using at a real
university. I also wanted originality, so I asked whether my idea overlapped
with other students' projects, and started building as soon as I was sure it
didn't.

At the Student Hub, the missing informal contact between students and
academics is a known gap (Belinda mentioned it). I hope this project gets noticed
one day and becomes something solid enough to be used in the real Student
Hub. That's the developer I want to be: one who builds things that are
actually used.
